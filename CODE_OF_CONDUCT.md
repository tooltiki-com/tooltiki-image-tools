# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

The short version: be decent to people. Assume the person on the other end is
acting in good faith, keep criticism aimed at the code, and take the hint when
someone asks you to drop something.

Report anything that needs reporting to **info@tooltiki.com**. Reports are read
by the maintainers at TecWeb B.V. and handled privately.
